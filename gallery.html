The Silmarils

Fëanor crafted these three great jewels, capturing the inner fire and blended light of the Trees of Valinor.

The Elder Wand

The most powerful wand in wizarding history, forged from elder wood with a Thestral tail-hair core.

Sting

An Elven shortsword forged in Gondolin. It glows with a blue luminescence when Orcs are near.

The One Ring

Forged in the fires of Mount Doom by Sauron to control the bearers of the other Rings of Power.

The Marauder's Map

A magical document revealing all of Hogwarts School, including every secret passage and person's location.

Phial of Galadriel

A crystal phial containing the light of Eärendil's star, given to Frodo Baggins to light dark places.
