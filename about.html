The Elves call the great spirits the Valar, who are the Powers of Arda, while Men often refer to them as gods[cite: 4]. The hierarchy consists of seven Lords of the Valar and seven Queens, known as the Valier[cite: 4]. Manwë and Melkor were originally brethren in the thought of Ilúvatar, but Manwë understands Ilúvatar's purposes most clearly[cite: 4].

The darkest of magical arts. A Horcrux is an object in which a Dark wizard has hidden a fragment of their soul to attain immortality.

Forged in the Second Age of Middle-earth, these rings were intended to preserve the Elven realms. However, the Dark Lord Sauron forged the One Ring to rule them all.

A cataclysmic event in *A Song of Ice and Fire* that shattered the Valyrian peninsula in a single day. It wiped out the dragonlords, their ancient magic, and their empire, leaving only the Targaryens as survivors.

A multiversal cataclysm in *The Witcher* universe occurring 1,500 years ago. It trapped unnatural creatures like ghouls, vampires, and humans in the realm of the Elves, fundamentally altering the fabric of reality.

In *The Wheel of Time*, this apocalypse was caused by male channelers driven mad by the Dark One's taint on the True Source. They literally tore the earth apart, shifting oceans and flattening mountain ranges.

The foundational laws of *The Chronicles of Narnia*, inscribed on the Stone Table. It dictates that every traitor belongs to the White Witch, though the Deeper Magic from before the dawn of time offers a path to resurrection.

A galaxy-wide crusade in the *Dune* universe against computers, thinking machines, and conscious robots. This resulted in the strict commandment: "Thou shalt not make a machine in the likeness of a human mind."

A Sith philosophy established by Darth Bane in *Star Wars*. To prevent internal destruction and consolidate dark side power, there must only ever be two Sith at a time: a master to embody power, and an apprentice to crave it.

The language of dragons and magic in *Earthsea*. To know a thing or person's true name in the Old Speech grants a wizard absolute power over it, making true names closely guarded secrets.

A magical disaster in *Dungeons & Dragons* (Forgotten Realms) caused by the assassination of Mystra, the Goddess of Magic. Blue flames swept across the world, mutating creatures and fracturing the very laws of magic.

In *The Elder Scrolls*, the entire race of advanced, subterranean Elves (Dwarves) vanished simultaneously during the Battle of Red Mountain after their Chief Tonal Architect struck the Heart of Lorkhan.

The catastrophic war in *Elden Ring* triggered when Queen Marika shattered the titular ring. Her demigod children claimed the shards, initiating an endless, stalemate war that left the Lands Between in ruin.

Din, Nayru, and Farore created the realm of Hyrule in *The Legend of Zelda*. Upon departing for the heavens, they left behind the Triforce, a sacred relic granting the ultimate wish of whoever touches it.

A parallel dimension in *Avatar: The Last Airbender* inhabited by immortal spirits. Only the Avatar serves as the great bridge between the physical and spiritual realms to maintain balance.

Cyclical, apocalyptic wars in *The Stormlight Archive* fought between the human Knights Radiant and the Voidbringers. Each Desolation pushed human civilization back to the Stone Age.

In *The Sandman*, the Endless are seven powerful beings older than gods: Destiny, Death, Dream, Destruction, Desire, Despair, and Delirium. They are the anthropomorphic embodiments of universal concepts.

The giant star turtle of Terry Pratchett's *Discworld*. A'Tuin swims through the cosmos carrying four giant elephants (Berilia, Tubul, Great T'Phon, and Jerakeen), who in turn carry the flat, circular world.

A massive explosion ten thousand years ago in *Warcraft* that destroyed the Well of Eternity. It split the ancient supercontinent of Kalimdor into the fragmented continents of Azeroth as they exist today.

A mysterious, conscious elementary particle in *His Dark Materials*. It is drawn to human consciousness and experience, viewed by the oppressive Magisterium as the physical manifestation of original sin.

A terrible winter in *A Song of Ice and Fire* that lasted a generation. The White Walkers descended from the deep north, bringing famine and death until they were driven back during the Battle for the Dawn.

A lethal, encroaching mist in the *Mistborn* series that threatened to destroy the world. The Lord Ruler took the power of the Well of Ascension to defeat it, inadvertently turning the world into an ash-covered wasteland.

A legendary, ancient conflict in *The Kingkiller Chronicle* fought between the Shapers, who sought to master and change reality, and the Namers, who understood and respected the natural order of the world.
