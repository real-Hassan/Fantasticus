The Elves call the great spirits the Valar, who are the Powers of Arda, while Men often refer to them as gods[cite: 4]. The hierarchy consists of seven Lords of the Valar and seven Queens, known as the Valier[cite: 4]. Manwë and Melkor were originally brethren in the thought of Ilúvatar, but Manwë understands Ilúvatar's purposes most clearly[cite: 4].
