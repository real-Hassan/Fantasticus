Chronicles of the First Age & Beyond

Welcome, seeker of hidden knowledge. This compendium safeguards the highest of fantasy lore, bridging the vast gulf between the Elder Days of Middle-earth and the hidden magical societies of the modern world.

Within these pages, you will find accounts of the creation of Arda and the secrets of Hogwarts. We chronicle the tragic oath of Fëanor, the journey of Bilbo Baggins in *The Hobbit*, the treacherous history of the Deathly Hallows, and the ancient magics that bind both realms.
